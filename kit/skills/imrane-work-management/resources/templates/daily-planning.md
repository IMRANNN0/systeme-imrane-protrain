# Daily planning in one project

Date / selected lane / available capacity:

| Item | Impact 1–5 | Urgency 1–5 | Effort 1–5 | Reason and confidence | Owner | Blocker | Next action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Work item | 3 | 3 | 2 | Explain ratings | Responsible person | Missing dependency or none | Specific action |

Recommendation: up to three actionable priorities using impact × urgency ÷ effort as a transparent starting point. Separate blocked work and name the unblock action. Respect actual deadlines, commitments and capacity; ask the owner when a material tradeoff needs their choice. Do not silently reprioritize work or aggregate private client content here.

End of day: record completed result, evidence, material blocker and next action. Review elapsed time and revisions on comparable tasks rather than rewarding task count alone.
