# Marketing Reporting

Create a report that answers a defined business question and lets its reader distinguish measured results, uncertainty and proposed next work. Use the simplest useful format: a column specification, concise performance summary, table, dashboard or recurring-report template.

## Scope and routing

- Work in the selected lane with its authorized sources. Keep raw data, report files, mappings and evidence in that lane; never put client data in shared skills. Preserve raw inputs, minimize personal data and omit personal identifiers from summary reports unless necessary and authorized.
- Report creation does not authorize connecting sources, external uploads/indexing, publication, contact, spending, campaign/account edits or external sharing. Prepare a report or configuration specification; apply a live change only within separate task authorization.
- Use [Data Analysis Process](../media-buying-data-analysis-process/SKILL.md) for collection, cleaning and joins when needed; [Analysis Flow](../media-buying-analysis-flow/SKILL.md) for a diagnostic investigation; and [Monetization](../media-buying-monetization/SKILL.md) for cost coverage or financial targets. A factual report can remain useful with an inconclusive diagnosis.
- This skill converts only the initial **Reporting** material received from the owner on 5 November 2026. Templates and examples are local operational adaptations. Further reporting details, separate sector tracks, fallacies and techniques, and macro/micro funnels are not fully supplied; do not claim full-course coverage.

## Build for the decision

Read [the reporting protocol](references/reporting-protocol.md) for on-platform columns, naming, multi-source reconciliation and calculation rules. Use [the templates and examples](references/templates-and-examples.md) for an actionable output.

1. **Set the report contract.** Reuse known objective, audience, decision, scope, frequency, comparison, segment and target provenance. Define source/refresh time, reporting period/timezone, currency, grain, cohort/status/event definitions, attribution and coverage. If inputs are missing, deliver a specification with missing fields marked unknown instead of inventing results.
2. **Choose relevant columns and sections.** Start with the business-aligned primary metric, then its compatible diagnostics and volumes. The owner's e-commerce example uses ROAS/AOV/CPS/CVR/CPC and spend/orders/revenue/sessions. These are diagnostic and volume views; their labels do not establish profitability or scalability. For leads, services or affiliate work select meaningful qualified, mature, retained or approved/paid outcomes instead of forcing ROAS.
3. **Prepare comparable data.** Document stable-ID/category mappings and unknowns. Reconcile sources on defensible keys and grain; check join cardinality and control totals. Keep incompatible attribution views separate, and never sum overlapping platform-attributed sales as actual business revenue.
4. **Calculate and present clearly.** Recompute ratios from eligible numerator/denominator totals. Use compatible funnel transitions only; distinguish clicks, page views, sessions, users, events and orders. Charts and heat maps need explicit units, periods, coverage and scales. Show target provenance and uncertainty; a daily pattern localizes timing without establishing cause.
5. **Separate evidence from interpretation.** State observations, competing explanations, limitations, business impact and next validation separately. Record owner and next step when known. No report automatically establishes causality, net profit, a winner or a safe scaling decision.

Before delivery, reconcile important totals and filters to the source, verify calculations and mapping coverage, mark missing/stale/provisional data and attribution/maturity differences, and state residual limitations. Do not claim an automatic or live refresh unless it has actually been tested. Present estimates, targets and forecasts as such.
