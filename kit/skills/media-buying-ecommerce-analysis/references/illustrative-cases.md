# Synthetic illustrative cases

All figures are invented in arbitrary currency units (CU). They are method checks, not course rules, client results or benchmarks.

## A booked ROAS of five becomes retained ROAS of three

A mature, fully attributed acquisition cohort costs CU 3,000. It produces 150 created orders with CU 15,000 booked gross value; 30 cancel before shipping. Of 120 shipped orders, 108 deliver and 12 fail delivery. Of 108 delivered orders, 18 return in full and 90 are retained and paid. Each order has one unit; all failed/returned units are recovered into saleable inventory in this simplified example. Net retained sales are CU 9,000, already excluding discounts, refunds and tax.

| Result | Calculation |
| --- | --- |
| Pre-shipping cancellation | 30 / 150 = 20% |
| Delivery failure | 12 / 120 = 10% of shipped orders |
| Post-delivery return | 18 / 108 = 16.67% of delivered orders |
| Retained share of created orders | 90 / 150 = 60% |
| Created-order CPS | 3,000 / 150 = CU 20 |
| Retained-order CPS | 3,000 / 90 = CU 33.33 |
| Retained net AOV | 9,000 / 90 = CU 100 |
| Booked gross value / spend | 15,000 / 3,000 = 5, labeled booked gross ROAS |
| Retained net revenue ROAS | 9,000 / 3,000 = 3 |

Calling 18/150 the post-delivery return rate changes its denominator. It can be labeled the returned share of created orders (12%), but it answers another question. The 108 delivered historical events include the 18 later returns; do not add the two counts as exclusive current states.

Costs are CU 3,600 retained-unit COGS, CU 600 outbound delivery (120 x 5), CU 72 return handling (18 x 4), CU 36 failed-delivery handling (12 x 3), CU 270 payment costs and CU 240 packaging (120 x 2). These cost categories are distinct and complete only for the stated scope.

Contribution before media = 9,000 - 3,600 - 600 - 72 - 36 - 270 - 240 = CU 4,182. Contribution after media = CU 1,182. No additional full COGS charge is invented for recovered stock. Fixed overhead and other omitted costs remain unknown; this is not net profit. The pre-media contribution ratio is 4,182/9,000 = 46.47%; under unchanged economics its break-even revenue ROAS is 9,000/4,182 = 2.15, a conditional arithmetic boundary rather than proof of safe scale.

## Returns maturity can hide deterioration

Cohort A has 30 days of return observation and cohort B has three. B's higher apparent retained ROAS may disappear after outstanding deliveries and returns settle. Report unresolved volume and observation ages. Compare equal-age histories if available; do not mark B a winner or invent final refunds. If the return model is unknown, the mature comparison is unavailable.

## Platform overlap does not create store revenue

For one calendar period, platform X attributes CU 8,000 and platform Y CU 6,000, while the store records CU 10,000 net sales. This does not prove tracking loss or CU 14,000 actual sales. The two platforms can claim the same order and use different attribution windows. Reconcile order IDs/windows/tax/refund definitions where possible; otherwise keep the three views separate. Store sales also need attribution evidence before being divided by a particular campaign's spend as its ROAS.
