These guidelines are production decisions, not measured campaign-performance claims.

## Message and audience

Preserve the exact submitted script. Suggest wording changes separately and obtain permission before applying them. The student's decision problem is which formations to choose for their academic profile. Show the relationship between profile, proposed formation and explanation. An attractive university name or generic study photograph alone does not explain it.

Keep Company and Match distinct. Real agency successes must not be presented as outcomes caused by Match. No invented admissions, testimonials, precise probabilities, deadlines or features. Use authentic product recordings for any scene described as a Match demonstration. An explicitly labelled explanatory diagram can support understanding but cannot replace authentic UI in a claimed product demo.

## Picture, motion and editing

Use generated raster images when they serve the beat; the owner explicitly permits them. Combine them with cleared footage, authentic product captures and controlled graphics as needed. Code is for assembly, precise type, motion and explanatory relationships, not a requirement to draw every detailed asset.

For substantial work, build key visual frames and a rough animatic after storyboard approval and any applicable paid-service authorization, before polishing. Break down any reference's story, information reveal, motion, framing, sound and ending; record the actual viewing scope. Kresco remains a desired reference direction, not a verified exact production technique or copied identity.

Each cut/reveal should change the idea, show evidence or guide attention. Avoid long stretches of decorative zoom over unrelated images. Calm stills and hard cuts are valid when intentional. There is no required transition count, compulsory kinetic text or music/effects quota. Reuse a coherent visual treatment rather than accumulating unrelated AI scenes.

Hold product details long enough to read. Redact student names, contacts and private identifiers in image and audio before use. Preserve conditions and relevant qualifiers when cropping a report. Never invent formation names/eligibility rules to fill a missing recording.

## Darija voice and captions

Aim for conversational Moroccan Darija, clear Campus France/ProTrain Match pronunciation and unforced pauses. The brand's accent/delivery needs actual review; a model claiming authenticity is not native human approval. Use a generic permitted voice, never a cloned Hajj/student voice without explicit authorization.

Lock the text before TTS. Select pronunciation annotations without silently changing the approved words. Measure the recording, then refine the timeline; do not unnaturally speed or slow speech to hit an estimated55 seconds. The storyboard is a screen-time budget until actual audio exists.

Caption the actual narration, with sentence/phrase boundaries and measured timings. Transcription utilities do not prove Darija accuracy. Use manually corrected captions for the pilot if ASR/alignment is not validated. Save SRT/CaptionJSON and burned-in Arabic preview. Visual headings and captions may complement one another; avoid unreadable duplication.

## Music and sound

Plan sound with picture. Prioritize speech, complete sentence tails and clear pauses. Add licensed music/ambience or purposeful SFX only when they support this script. No paid library, generative music service or external processing without the required authorization. Preserve source/rights information; do not treat a downloaded/trending track as cleared for ads.

Check the actual mix at normal speed and the actual per-channel peaks. Loudness normalization and waveform similarity do not validate accent or natural delivery. Document whether a reviewer listened, a model assessed audio, or only signal measurements ran.

## Proposed export profile

For the pilot:1080×1920,9:16,30 fps,H264MP4,AAC48 kHz, readable captions and fast-start playback. Target standard8-bit4:2:0 and a verified SDR color workflow; the earlier full-range/bt470bg export does not establish a tested Rec709 delivery. Define/test conversion against actual source material before claiming color correctness. Bitrate/CRF should suit the footage and legible UI; avoid repeated lossy re-encoding.

This is a proposed delivery profile, not certified Meta acceptance. Check current intended placement preview and the exact final MP4 before publication. Remotion production licensing and media rights must be resolved where applicable; no purchase is authorized by choosing a codec.
