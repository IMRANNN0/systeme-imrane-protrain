## Camera, lighting and editing

- Framing: shot size medium shot 100%; subject is the in-focus region (no face; shot size is an estimate)
- Camera: fixed camera (tripod / mount / on the desk) 100%
- Lighting: soft frontal light (flat light). Key light frontal (from the camera side; both sides of the subject about equally bright), from above (ceiling light / street lamp / high source); lighting ratio about 1.1:1 (0.1 stops); soft light (almost no lit / shadow edge); colour temperature neutral (about 5000K); background slightly soft
- (not live footage: the above describes the light in the picture, for generation prompts, not a shooting plan)
- Grade: natural (close to the original colours); normal saturation, normal contrast, normal blacks, overall warm
- Editing: 6.3 cuts per minute, 7.6s average shot; no live-action shots
