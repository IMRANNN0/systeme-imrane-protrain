# Static image ad review checklist

Use before production and again after inspecting the actual exports. Record evidence and unresolved issues; apply the checks relevant to the task without adding approval gates or fixed scores.

## Before production

- Read the current local brief, brand sources, claim sources, owner decisions, and durable lessons. Distinguish rejected work, technical passes, and owner-approved improvements.
- Identify the audience situation and the hook's relevant problem, question, or desire. Would the hook fit almost any advertiser unchanged?
- State the promise as a specific, realistic benefit. Identify its supporting product or approved claim source and its relationship to the landing page. Flag missing or conflicting evidence; do not fill gaps with invented benefits, results, admission guarantees, or testimonials.
- Confirm that the CTA names one clear next action matching the real destination, funnel stage, and offer conditions. Separate a free entry step from any later paid outcome.
- Explain how the visual communicates the product mechanism, relevant evidence, or audience situation. Avoid generic educational-poster treatment and template decoration without a communication job. The three messaging functions do not prescribe three visual regions or any particular motif.
- Distinguish illustration from product proof. Obtain genuine captures before presenting UI, reports, recommendations, or results as actual product output.
- Plan a mobile-first hierarchy with short, natural copy in the project's chosen language and tone. Use original supplied branding and suitable editable text layers.
- For mixed scripts, plan Arabic shaping and French LTR runs, intended reading order, manual fragments or breaks where needed, and punctuation placement. Do not treat one earlier layout's line count or punctuation arrangement as a universal constraint.

## After the actual export

- Inspect full-size and realistic phone-size exports, not source strings alone. Record the artifact path, hash, date, dimensions, and source assets. Do not report visual checks as passed when no export was inspected.
- Read the hook, promise, and CTA in their visible order. Check whether the first read is clear, the promise remains readable, and the action is distinct. Check image dominance, competing text weights, contrast, spacing, safe areas, and clipping.
- Compare visible wording to approved copy. Manually inspect mixed-script fragments, Arabic connections, French word order, line breaks, and punctuation attachments at both sizes. A logically exact string does not prove correct visual reading order.
- Verify original branding is intact and every proof-like element is genuine or clearly illustrative. Recheck claims, offer terms, and CTA against their sources and destination.
- Revisit local lessons and report actual failures, limitations, and explicitly approved improvements. Preserve historical evidence and narrow the lesson to its observed scope.
- Keep factual technical/creative review, owner approval, and measured performance as separate statuses. A guideline approval is not creative approval; visual judgment does not establish a winning ad or conversion performance.
